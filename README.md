## About the Game

Meet **Felpudo**, a brave little pilot with a big heart and a lot of fluff! In **Roof Rescue: Sky Mission**, simple mechanics meet classic arcade dogfighting action. 

Your mission? Take control of Felpudo's airplane and patrol the skies under pressure. Destroy the remaining enemies to reach the final objective. The path to victory isn't straightforward—you will need to fend off waves of quirky balloon-slug enemies, manage your mission quota, survive the ticking clock, and face down the ultimate threat: the fearsome final boss, **Uruca**! You will need to hit him 10 times to reach the battle's victory, while carefully dodging fireballs. Remember, Uruca is not happy with your presence! 

---

## Key Features

* **Classic Arcade Action:** Easy to pick up and play, focusing on fun shooting and movement mechanics.
* **Intense Boss Battles:** Survive waves of enemies to trigger the boss fight against the menacing Uruca, complete with dynamic attack patterns and health tracking.
* **Dynamic Feedback:** Enjoy visual feedback like smooth damage flashing, dynamic UI counters, and a sleek victory cutscene sequence.
* **Heartwarming Goal:** Clear the skies, defeat Uruca, and guide Felpudo to a triumphant victory!
* **Controlls:** Use the right click in your mouse or press the Alt key to fly. Press spacebar for shooting bullets.

---

## How to Play

1. **Take Flight:** Use your mouse or flight controls to navigate your airplane across the skies.
2. **Eliminate Threats:** Shoot down incoming balloon-slug enemies to meet your quota and trigger the boss encounter.
3. **Defeat the Boss:** Face Uruca, dodge incoming projectile patterns, and land your hits to defeat the ultimate threat.
4. **Controls:** 
   * **Left Mouse Button / Fire:** Ascend / Fly / Interact
   * **Spacebar:** Fire projectiles
   * **R:** Restart game upon victory or defeat
